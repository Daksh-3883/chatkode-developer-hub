# Add the Human-First AI Philosophy Section

## Goal
Add one new section near the end of the existing ChatKode page, after the Kode Developers section and before the final call to action. Preserve every existing section, the logo, page structure, and the approved dark technical design.

## What will be added
- A prominent statement: “Not just a capable AI. An AI with boundaries.”
- Concise supporting copy explaining that ChatKode is intended to help people build, learn, reason, and solve problems while respecting human agency and appropriate boundaries.
- A compact, accessible HUMAN → CHATKODE → HUMAN DECISION system showing direction, assistance, review, and responsibility.
- Four principles: Human first; Know your limits; Help, don’t control; Use it responsibly.
- A small “Capability ≠ Authority” comparison that distinguishes useful technical capability from permission to decide for or control people.
- A calm responsibility close: Kode Developers builds the tool; people decide how they use it. “Build with it. Think with it. Verify when it matters.”
- A short final CTA line connecting capability with responsibility without turning the CTA into a warning.
